Our Journey & Foundation

Your Trusted Healthcare Partner in Mayiladuthurai

Vairam Multi Speciality Hospital was established in 2010 to fill the gap for world-class, affordable clinical treatments in Mayiladuthurai, Sirkazhi, Karaikal, and Kumbakonam. Our facility houses advanced diagnostic labs and modern cleanroom operation theatres managed by the best doctor specialists.

We combine cutting-edge medical technologies with personalized attention to ensure every patient experiences a warm, recovery-focused journey.

Our Vision

To be the leading, most trusted clinical provider in Tamil Nadu known for clinical excellence, ethics, and patient success rates.

Our Mission

To deliver advanced diagnostic treatment and emergency medical services using state-of-the-art technologies with warmth, honesty, and integrity.

Our Milestones

Historical Journey & Growth

From our humble beginnings to a leading multi-speciality medical centre in Tamil Nadu.

2010

Foundation & Launch

Vairam Hospital opens its doors in Mayiladuthurai with comprehensive general medicine and diagnostic laboratory capacities.

2015

Cardiology & Trauma Care Additions

Inaugurated the 24/7 Emergency & ICU Trauma unit, alongside custom senior cardiologist consulting lines for cardiac recovery.

2020

Advanced Modular Operation Theatres

Upgraded surgical infrastructure to modular cleanroom standards, adding arthroscopic orthopedic surgery suites and neurology critical care blocks.

2026

Digital Health Integration

Launched digital health checkups, corporate wellness integrations, and our online patient booking engine to serve Kumbakonam, Sirkazhi, and Karaikal.
